Stroke Play

Format & Rules


If you cannot find your or your partners ball in under 3 minutes you must drop latterally from where you and your group think the ball is lost.

All penalties are 1 additional stroke.

If you go into the water you may drop before the water with a 1 stroke penalty.

You get one Drivers club length not towards the hole on any shot. You may not change your Lie.

If you go into the cart path you get relief plus one Drivers club length not towards the hole.

No Mulligans allowed.

No gimmes allowed. Due to the nature of the tournament, all shots must be played as hit.
